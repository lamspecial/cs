01 — Problem

Marketing spend in Saudi Arabia is largely unaccountable, and the people best placed to sell are locked out of earning.

Saudi Arabia has one of the world's highest social media penetration rates, yet businesses pay for impressions with no guarantee of a real customer walking through the door.
Over 700,000 registered SMEs operate in the Kingdom — the majority rely on word-of-mouth but have no structured way to reward it or measure its impact.
Millions of everyday social media users have engaged, loyal audiences but no accessible path to monetize their influence without being a verified macro-influencer.
Consumers distrust digital ads; they trust recommendations from people they follow. There is no platform that converts that trust into a verified, measurable commercial transaction.

02 — Solution

Shabih closes the loop: verified in-store referrals, powered by micro-ambassadors, settled in an internal economy.

No follower minimum required — the platform aggregates thousands of micro-audiences into collective commercial scale.
Internal credits are spendable across the partner ecosystem, creating a closed-loop economy that retains value on platform.

Worked example: 1,000 Shabeehs, 10 referrals each/month = 10,000 verified visits. Platform earns 100,000 credits in spread. At a 1 SAR/credit rate, that is 100,000 SAR monthly from referral spread alone.
Internal spending generates a second layer — 70% of credits spent internally doubles the spread per credit unit before it leaves the system.
Margin is embedded in every transaction; no ad budget required to grow revenue.

07 — Growth Strategy

Growth is driven by ambassador activity — not paid acquisition. Every Shabeeh is a distribution node.

Phase 1 — Prove the Loop (Months 1–6): Single district in Riyadh. Anchor in food, beauty, and wellness. Onboard 20–30 businesses and 500 Shabeehs. Measure referral-to-visit conversion rate and credit retention.
Phase 2 — Flywheel (Months 7–18): Expand to multiple Riyadh districts, then Jeddah and Dammam. Shabeehs recruit other Shabeehs through referral incentives. Growth becomes self-reinforcing.
Phase 3 — Open Platform (Month 19+): Expand across all business categories. Introduce tiered Shabeeh ranking system with status rewards. Launch GCC expansion starting with UAE.
